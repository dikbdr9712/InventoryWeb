Good to know

  • The same account. Sign in with the email and password you use on the website. Your cart, orders and notifications are the same everywhere.
  • Always up to date. New versions arrive by themselves. When one is ready, the app offers Update now.
  • Fast to open. The app keeps its pages on your phone. Ordering, paying and following orders still need the internet.
  • To remove it, press and hold the icon and choose Remove or Uninstall, like any app.
