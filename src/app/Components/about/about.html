About us

DP DrukBazaars is an online marketplace from Bhutan. We connect people with products they can trust, and with the local sellers and riders who bring them to their door.

Get in touch

Our mission

Our mission is to make online shopping in Bhutan simple, safe and reliable: easy ordering, safe payment from your own bank account, and delivery you can follow from start to finish. We help local businesses and makers reach more customers.

Our vision

Our vision is a marketplace where every local business in Bhutan can sell to customers across the country, and every customer can shop with confidence, wherever they live.
